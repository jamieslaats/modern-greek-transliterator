† av / af and ev / ef: αυ and ευ are voiced (/av/, /ev/) before vowels and voiced consonants, and voiceless (/af/, /ef/) before voiceless consonants (κ τ π σ φ θ χ ξ ψ). This is applied automatically.

* γ palatalisation: γ becomes y before front vowels (ε η ι ει οι). Before other vowels and consonants it remains g.

Note on θ and dh: θ (th) is the voiceless dental fricative as in thin. δ (dh) is the voiced dental fricative as in this. These are distinct sounds in Modern Greek.
